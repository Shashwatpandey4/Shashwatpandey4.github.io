About Me

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at AGI, Inc., building AI infrastructure across the systems stack.

My interests lie at the intersection of compilers, high-performance kernels, inference optimization, and distributed systems. I work on compiler and kernel development for CPU, GPU, and NPU architectures, backend infrastructure, and runtime systems that enable efficient large-scale AI workloads.

Previously, I conducted research in ML systems and compilers at Arizona State University and worked as a Data Engineer, building high-throughput distributed systems.

Core Technologies: C++, CUDA, LLVM/MLIR, Triton, Python, Distributed Systems, GPU Performance Optimization, AI Inference.
